Welcome to the Agentic AI Summit 2027, hosted by the Responsible Decentralized Intelligence Foundation and Berkeley RDI.

Building on the phenomenal success of the 2026 Agentic AI Summit, which brought together ~4,000 in-person attendees and 100,000+ virtual participants, we're thrilled to invite you to join us on August 20–21, 2027 in San Francisco for the next chapter of Agentic AI.

We're witnessing Agentic AI moving from research into production at unprecedented velocity—reshaping industries, creating new economic value, and raising urgent questions about safety, security, and responsible governance.

The 2027 summit will convene the most influential voices in the field: visionary leaders and researchers, pioneering entrepreneurs, leading AI organizations, venture capitalists, and policymakers. Together, we'll explore the cutting edge of agents’ capabilities, grapple with real-world deployment challenges, and collaboratively shape the future of safe, secure and beneficial Agentic AI.

What to Expect:

This will be the largest and most comprehensive gathering dedicated to Agentic AI—bringing together leaders across the full stack, from foundation models and agent frameworks to evaluation, infrastructure, safety, security, governance and large-scale deployment. The program will showcase groundbreaking research, diverse industry applications, and critical conversations on ensuring these systems remain safe, secure, and beneficial.

📍 Dates:   August 20–21, 2027

📍 Location:   San Francisco, CA

FAQ

01 Are visa invitation letters available for Agentic AI Summit attendees? +

Yes. Visa invitation letters are available only to registered Summit attendees.

To be eligible:

  1. You must first register for the Summit via Luma.
  2. After completing registration, you may submit a request through our Visa Invitation Letter Request Form.
  3. Our team will review your submission and follow up if additional information is required.

We recommend that international participants register and submit requests as early as possible to allow adequate processing time.

02 Do you offer nonprofit pricing, scholarships, fee waivers, or other discounts? +

We're unable to offer additional discounts, scholarships, or fee waivers beyond our current Early Bird ticket pricing, which is already significantly subsidized to maximize accessibility. Ticket prices help cover essential venue, programming, and operational costs so we can deliver a high-quality Summit experience.

03 Do you provide travel, flight, or hotel assistance or recommendations? +

We do not provide travel or flight assistance, arrange hotel accommodations, or offer travel or lodging recommendations or support. Attendees are responsible for making their own travel and lodging arrangements.

04 Is virtual attendance available? +

Yes, a livestream option will be available at no cost. Information on how to register for virtual attendance will be shared closer to the event.

05 How can I get a receipt of purchase? +

You can find your payment history here: luma.com/settings/payment

You can also get here by going to your Luma Settings → Payment tab. You can see your payment history on both the Luma website and the iOS app.

Click on the three dots to the right of the tickets you want receipts for, and select Download Receipt or Download Invoice.

You will be able to add information to your receipt, like your Billing Address, Tax Information, and any other notes.

You can then use your browser's settings (Command + P on Mac, or Control + P on Windows) to print out the receipt/invoice or save it as a PDF.

06 How do I cancel my registration? +

All registrations and cancellations are handled directly through Luma, our ticketing platform. After registering, you should have received a confirmation email containing your ticket details. From that email, you can click the “Event Page” button to visit the Agentic AI Summit page. On the event page, just below the “Add to Calendar” button, you’ll see the option to “Notify the host by canceling your registration.”

If you cancel within the terms of our refund policy, your registration will be eligible for a refund.

07 I just canceled my registration – when will I receive my refund? +

Refunds are typically processed within 3 business days after a cancellation is completed. Depending on your payment method, it may take additional time for the funds to appear on your statement. You will receive an email notification when the refund has been issued.

08 How can I correct information if I made a mistake when registering for the event? +
  1. Please re-register at luma.com/agentic-ai-summit with the correct information.
  2. We will then process a refund (minus the processing fee) to your original payment method for the incorrect registration.
